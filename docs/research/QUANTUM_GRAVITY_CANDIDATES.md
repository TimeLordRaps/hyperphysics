## Interior geometry

* `[FORM]` Schwarzschild black-hole interior: maximal volume grows linearly in time, finite at each moment, unbounded over time ([Christodoulou–Rovelli](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.2854)). The classical singularity is a moment in the future, not a shell.
* `[FORM]` Coleman–De Luccia bubble: finite bubble, open-FRW interior with hyperbolic slices and infinite spatial volume ([Census Taker's Hat](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.1129)). Closest match to "finite exterior, infinite interior".
* Baby universe (small throat, large closed inflating interior; Frolov–Markov–Mukhanov, Farhi–Guth): *recalled*.
* Gravity operates throughout these GR solutions; only at a true singularity do the laws fail, which is the quantum-gravity problem. Nothing established says a singularity holds all possibilities at once.

## Wheeler–DeWitt and Hartle–Hawking

* Canonical quantization gives `ĤΨ = 0` for a wave function of a 3-geometry: no external time, the problem of time ([Quantum cosmology](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0610121)). Solved in minisuperspace. Open-FRW bubble interiors are homogeneous; the black-hole interior is Kantowski–Sachs, solved with wave functions regular across the interior ([arXiv 1510.00887](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.00887)): model results.
* Hartle–Hawking: path integral over compact Euclidean geometries; imaginary time smooths off the initial singularity. Hawking–Turok obtain open inflation ([paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0370269398002342)); their singular instanton is disputed ([hep-th/9803084](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9803084)); Feldbrugge–Lehners–Turok find the saddle unstable in a Lorentzian formulation ([1705.00192](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.00192)). Unsettled.
* Imaginary time is a calculational device for the classically forbidden region (`t → −iτ` makes the action real).
* **Objection, stated once:** nothing found connects imaginary time to transfinite or fractal structure; in Hartle–Hawking it removes a singularity, opposite to nesting singularities. "Requires imaginary time" stays `[OPEN]` until a mapping is stated.
* Bridges proposed (`[HYPER]`): discrete scale invariance with ratio `λ` between nesting levels gives complex exponents `a^(s + 2πin/ln λ)` and log-periodic oscillation (*recalled*); nesting as a fixed point `Ψ = F[Ψ]` of a coarse-graining map; periodic imaginary time as a thermal circle with winding as nesting.

## Non-uniqueness and the semiclassical limit

* Thiemann-style regularization leaves free: operator ordering, a half-integer representation label `m`, and whether the constraint creates nodes or only changes valence ([ambiguities](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0509118)). Anomaly-freedom is unsettled; the master-constraint programme ([Dittrich–Thiemann](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0411140)) works in models.
* Semiclassical limit at three levels: states (coherent states, weaves), large spin on a fixed graph (Regge gravity), dynamics (solutions of the constraint peaking on Einstein solutions: open).
* **REPRODUCED 2026-10-05**, `scripts/check_ponzano_regge_6j.py`: Ponzano–Regge asymptotics of the regular-tetrahedron SU(2) 6j, relative error 2.6% at j=5, 0.098% at j=20, 0.030% at j=40. One 3D toy, no local degrees of freedom; a first attempt with the interior dihedral angle failed (exterior is correct). The 4D EPRL case is not covered.
* Working backwards is many-to-one: the object is the equivalence class of foams that coarse-grain to the same geometry (the same quotient as the bisimulation of hyperreality's sempiternity criterion). The continuum limit is cylindrical consistency, a background-independent renormalization flow ([2211.09578](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.09578), [Bahr–Dittrich](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physics/articles/10.3389/fphy.2020.00295/full)); the self-similar attractor already exists there as a fixed point.
* Where ordinal indexing fits: two limits, large spin and graph refinement, taken in order; order matters when they do not commute. That is `limit labels` in this repository (word of stages, label `ω^n`; see `FIELD_STACK.md` and the order-of-limits code).
