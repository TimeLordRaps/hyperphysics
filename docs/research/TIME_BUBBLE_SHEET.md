# The time-bubble sheet: what physics allows, and what the family already holds

Owner's proposal (USER-STATED, 2026-10-05): oreality can be represented by arealities in prealities in infinite-time finite spaces; a time bubble whose boundary is a sheet of infinitesimally thin singularities, like a bubble's film, lets a finite space exist infinitely long inside itself and lets computational information cross the sheet. `[HYPER]` Retrieved 2026-10-05 unless marked *recalled*.

## Four distinct claims inside it

1. **The film is a singularity.** In general relativity a thin shell is not one. The Israel (Darmois–Israel) junction conditions keep the metric continuous across the shell and let the extrinsic curvature jump; the jump equals the shell's surface stress-energy (Lanczos equation) ([overview](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/darmois-israel-junction-formalism)). Curvature is a delta function, finite surface energy, and geodesics cross it. A genuine singular sheet, with divergent curvature and a degenerate metric, would not carry a signal through at all. So "sheet" should mean a regular thin shell, or a horizon. `[FORM]` for the first; the rest is the proposal.
2. **Finite space, infinite time.** The real instance is the de Sitter static patch: a horizon of finite area, so finitely many states, and unbounded static time (*recalled*; Poincaré recurrence time of order `e^S`). Bounds: Bekenstein `S ≤ 2πER/(ħc)`; covariant (Bousso) bound, entropy on a non-expanding light sheet of a surface is at most a quarter of its area ([entropy bounds](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0203101), [Bousso](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.5635)). Finite states plus infinite time gives recurrence, not new computation: the finite-state verdict of `wiki/hypersphere-and-preality.md`. `[FORM]`
3. **Infinite interior time against finite exterior time.** Redshift cannot supply it. A clock deeper in a potential runs slower than one outside, so a shell with a flat interior has interior time elapsing *less*. The needed asymmetry has to come from causal structure: the exterior's time ends or is cut off (pinch-off, horizon) while the interior goes on. That is a baby universe, a small throat to a large inflating interior (Frolov–Markov–Mukhanov, Farhi–Guth, *recalled*), with no return. `[FRAME]`
4. **"Arrive right after we left."** This needs the interior's infinite history to be readable at one exterior event. Two models, with different strength:
   * Malament–Hogarth (MH) spacetimes: an event whose past contains a worldline of infinite elapsed time, so an observer can receive the outcome of an infinite computation in finite time ([SEP, Supertasks](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/spacetime-supertasks/)). Etesi and Németi analysed a subclass containing rotating (Kerr) black holes and showed relations beyond Turing computability can be decided ([paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0104023)). Whether physically realizable (stability, signal strength) is contested. This is the mechanism for "translating information across the sheet": the receiver crosses at the MH event.
   * Closed timelike curves: polynomial-size CTC computers have exactly the power of PSPACE, classical or quantum ([Aaronson–Watrous](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.2669)). That is "arrive before you left" but not infinite-time power.

So the infinite-time-computer reading and the "return right after" reading use different resources (MH limit stage versus CTC fixed point). `[FORM]` on each model's stated result; which one the sheet is `[OPEN]`.

## Quantum computation in infinite time, and recurrence (USER-STATED 2026-10-05, then analysed)

Stated: the sheet "allows us to determine recurrent computations with quantum computation in infinite time." `[HYPER]` Three readings, kept apart.

* **R1, recurrence is guaranteed.** For unitary evolution on a finite-dimensional space, the state returns within any `ε` of its start infinitely often (quantum recurrence, *recalled*; the same almost-periodicity the numeric check uses). Infinite time supplies it with no further machinery. What infinite time adds is that the return can be waited for however long it takes; the exterior learning of it needs the hand-off of the previous section. `[FORM]`
* **R2, the limit stage is a recurrence detector.** The infinite-time Turing machine's rule at a limit stage is limsup per cell: a cell is 1 at `ω` exactly when it is 1 cofinally often, that is, when it recurs. For a quantum interior the analogue needs a rule, and a canonical one exists: the **time average** of the state, which for a finite-dimensional unitary converges to the diagonal ensemble `Σ_E P_E ρ P_E` (dephasing in the energy eigenspaces; mean ergodic theorem). This closes the open item "which limit rule at the limit stage" for a finite-dimensional quantum interior: Cesàro, which gives the dephased state. `scripts/check_quantum_recurrence.py` (REPRODUCED 2026-10-05, standard library, random 4-level spectrum): the largest off-diagonal entry of the average is `2.6e-3`, `3.5e-4`, `9.7e-5` at `10²`, `10³`, `10⁴` steps. Other rules (cluster set of the orbit, which is the whole orbit-closure torus, or the ordinal-remainder rule that returns the start) give different values; the choice is a postulate, now with a physically natural default. `[FRAME]`
* **R3, deciding recurrent computations.** For an interior with unbounded memory, deciding what recurs is what the infinite-time machine already does, classically. Finite-precision quantum computation is simulable by a classical machine with exponential overhead, so quantum adds no computability beyond classical in the same time model (standard, *recalled*). What quantum adds is **state-space size**: `n` qubits are a `2^n`-dimensional space, so an `S`-bit finite interior has a Hilbert space of dimension about `e^S`. The ε-return time on random spectra grows with dimension (same script, `ε = 0.1`, seven draws, medians of the first-return step: 1 for `d=2`, 2 for `d=3`, 14 for `d=4`, 49 for `d=5`; small sample, heuristic scaling `ε^-(d-1)` from a torus of dimension `d-1`). Extrapolated to `d ≈ e^S` that is doubly exponential in `S` (`[HYPER]`, not checked). Infinite time makes such waits free, which is the only sense in which quantum plus infinite time exceeds quantum in finite time. `[OPEN]` whether that is the owner's claim.

Limits: the check is one toy spectrum family; degenerate or rational spectra recur exactly and early; an interior with continuous observation (measurement) is not unitary and breaks R1.

The whole thing does not evolve; the system's state *conditioned on the clock reading `t`* is the `t`-th step of the computation. `scripts/check_history_state_clock.py` (REPRODUCED 2026-10-06, standard library, one qubit, `T = 5` random gates): `‖H h‖ = 2.4e-16`; conditional states match `U_t…U_1 ψ0` to `1.1e-16`; replacing one gate in `H` (negative control) gives `‖H' h‖ = 1.018`. `[FORM]` on the toy. This is the precise sense of "real time and null time entangled": the exterior's real-time clock is the register, the frozen interior is the system, and the computation exists only as their correlation. `[FRAME]` for the mapping.
* **Reconfiguration.** Changing one gate changes the program: the toy's reconfigured history state `h'` is again a zero mode of the new `H'` (`1.9e-16`), and its overlap with the old one is `0.3192`, so a reconfiguration is a different global state, not a perturbation of the old one. After an incorrigible commit `H` cannot be changed, so a reconfiguration has to be built in beforehand. The direct way to allow exactly one: add an exterior-held selector qubit `E` and use `H_total = H_A ⊗ |0><0|_E + H_B ⊗ |1><1|_E`; then `(|0>_E|h_A> + |1>_E|h_B>)/√2` is still a zero-energy state, and one measurement of `E` selects which computation the interior has been running, with `H` unchanged. That is one reconfiguration by one exterior bit (`[FORM]`, block-diagonal linear algebra, not run). Which bit, and when it is read, is the design.
* **Maintenance, and where "incorrigible" is real.** The history state is a zero mode only as long as `H` is the designed `H`; the gap above it is small for long computations (about `1/T²`, *recalled*, Kitaev–Shen–Vyalyi), so a generic perturbation moves it, and something must correct it. Here the demon is the computation: the syndrome measurement and correction are steps of `U_t` themselves, so the structure that is maintained is the structure that does the maintaining (a fixed point). That is the form of self-hosting the hypermath self-closure program studies, and it has the same limit: a mechanism cannot prove its own consistency (Gödel II), and "if its own maintenance can be proven then it holds" is Löb's schema, which yields the claim only given a proof from outside. `[FRAME]` Incorrigibility then means `H` and the selector are fixed and the exterior clock cannot be stopped from outside; it does not remove the model risk of the previous section. `[OPEN]`
* **Cost.** The correction records syndromes in the clock-correlated register; erasing records costs `kT ln 2` per bit (Landauer), paid on the real-time side because the interior has no time to spend it in. The demon is therefore powered and cooled from the exterior, which keeps the sheet a coupling. `[FRAME]`

## "An instant computation machine" (USER-STATED 2026-10-06, then analysed)

Stated: "So you get an instant computation machine because the internal nulltime is decidable instantly and by changing the outside the insides beginning and only point of computation occurs instantly in nulltime." `[HYPER]`

* **What is true.** In the history-state picture the interior has no time of its own and every step of the computation is present at once as a component of one stationary state; changing the exterior (the input boundary, the Hamiltonian, the selector bit) changes which global state that is. So the interior spends no time: its "beginning and only point" is the stationary state. `[FORM]` on the toy (previous section).
* **What it does not give: speed.** The time is not removed; it moves to the exterior. (1) Preparing the history state means reaching the ground (zero) state of the propagation Hamiltonian; its gap is exactly `2 − 2cos(π/(T+1)) ≈ π²/(T+1)²` (`scripts/check_instant_computation_cost.py`, REPRODUCED 2026-10-06: gap `0.267949, 0.022338, 0.001504` for `T = 5, 20, 80`, explicit eigenvector residual `~4e-16`), so adiabatic preparation takes at least of order `1/gap ≈ T²/π²` of exterior time (`1/gap = 3.7, 44.8, 664.9`); cooling into the state has no better general guarantee. `[FRAME]` on the adiabatic scale. (2) Reading the answer: the clock is found at the last step with probability `1/(T+1)` for the unpadded state; appending `T` idle steps lifts it to `(T+1)/(2T+1)` (`0.5455, 0.5122, 0.5031`), at double the clock length, and each readout destroys the state so it must be prepared again. Ground-state preparation of local Hamiltonians is QMA-hard in general (Kitaev, *recalled*), which is the reason no free speed-up is expected: a physical process that prepared such ground states quickly for every instance would give a computer stronger than the standard model, and that is the claim an "instant machine" would have to defend. `[OPEN]`
* **"Decidable instantly" mixes two things.** Decidability is about *which* problems can be solved, speed about *how fast*. A frozen interior performs a finite history of `T+1` steps (a normalizable state needs finite `T`), so it decides exactly what finite computations decide; it does not decide the halting problem however fast it is. Only the infinite-time reading (superseded by the owner on 2026-10-06 for this proposal) reached beyond that. `[FORM]`
* **Reading it charitably.** The honest version of the claim is a *relocation of cost*: interior time is zero; exterior time is polynomial in the interior's step count, plus the unmeasured cost of the real-time side maintaining the stationary state. That is already a precise, testable design statement, and the toy shows both numbers. `[FRAME]`

## Sempiternity's rules operate hyperlogically (USER-STATED 2026-10-06, then analysed)

Stated: "sempiternitys rules then operate hyperlogically". `[HYPER]` Read here as: inside the frozen interior there is no time to order events, so what orders them is derivation, which is the subject of hyperlogic (a derivation structure from `turn` under circuit closure) and of hypermath (a form as a reachable closed derivation chain from L0, with `derives` directional).

* **A precise bridge, proved and checked.** For the propagation Hamiltonian `H` of the history-state section and *any* clock-system state `ψ = (ψ_0, …, ψ_T)`, `⟨ψ|H|ψ⟩ = Σ_t ‖ψ_t − U_t ψ_{t−1}‖²`: the energy is the total squared violation of derivation steps (each `ψ_t` should be what rule `U_t` derives from `ψ_{t−1}`). So the zero-energy states are exactly the chains in which every step is valid, and such a chain is fixed by `ψ_0` alone, since `ψ_t = U_t ψ_{t−1}` is forced: derivation is directional, and the frozen interior's "dynamics" is the existence of a valid chain, not evolution in time. `scripts/check_derivation_energy.py` (REPRODUCED 2026-10-06, standard library): the identity holds on 200 random non-valid chains to `3.6e-15`; a valid chain has energy `~0`; replacing one `ψ_3` gives energy `0.7699`, equal to its summed violations; valid chains from different `ψ_0` differ (up to `1.247`). `[FORM]` for the identity and the determinism (two lines of algebra), the numbers are a check.
* **What a contradiction is here.** A broken step is a positive-energy component; energy measures how much of the chain is not derivable from its predecessors. Non-contradiction (the closedness condition on a form) is the zero-energy condition, and the corrective demon of the earlier sections is exactly what removes violations. `[FRAME]`
* **Unknown unknowns carry over.** The interior is a finite theory-like structure, so the GL result applies to it: `Con` of its own rules is true, unprovable from inside, and not provably unprovable (hyperlogic `tests/test_unknown_unknowns.py`). `[FRAME]`
* **What is not established.** Hyperlogic's own L0 states that it "is not a logic": no connective, quantifier, inference rule or proof object. The bridge above shows the interior's rules have the shape of a derivation structure; it does not show they are hyperlogic's L0 triangle (shape, matter, reading) or that L0 discharges any of this. The identification is a proposal. `[OPEN]`
