# The operational field stack

This file records the declared subject matter of hyperphysics and of the two
fields that stand above it. It is a **scope declaration**, not a derivation.
Read the distinction in "What this does not do" before citing it.

## The declaration

Declared by Tyler Roost, 2026-09-20, verbatim:

> "hyperbiology is for the future, its about the comprehensional meta structures
> of multi-combinatorial operations which are yielded by subpart operations,
> like how biology is that of chemistry. hyperchemistry will also exist, and is
> similarly the operation of operations within hyperphysics, hyperphysics is
> the mechanics, dynamics, and static features of operations."

## The stack

The three fields stand to one another as physics, chemistry and biology do, with
**operations** in the place matter occupies in the natural sciences.

| Field | Subject matter | Analogue | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hyperphysics** | the mechanics, dynamics, and static features **of operations** | physics | this repository; one layer written |
| **hyperchemistry** | the **operation of operations** within hyperphysics | chemistry | declared, not written |
| **hyperbiology** | the **comprehensional meta structures of multi-combinatorial operations yielded by subpart operations** | biology | declared; has a public repository, nothing written in it |

The ordering is the same one the natural sciences have, and it carries the same
two properties:

1. **Each tier's objects are the previous tier's composites.** Chemistry studies
   what atoms do in combination; hyperchemistry studies what operations do in
   combination. Biology studies structures whose behaviour is not read off any
   single molecule; hyperbiology studies structures whose behaviour is not read
   off any single operation.
2. **Each tier is not reducible in practice to the one below.** Stating that
   biology is chemistry does not let anyone do biology. The same caution applies
   here, and it is why these are separate fields rather than sections.

The word carrying the most weight in the hyperbiology clause is
**comprehensional**: the structures are not merely large combinations, they are
combinations *taken as a comprehended whole*. A multi-combinatorial structure
that is only enumerated is hyperchemistry's object. One that is grasped as a
unit, with features belonging to the unit rather than to its parts, is
hyperbiology's.

## What this means for this repository

`README.md` states that **hyperphysics' ground is not written**, and
`electrical.hm` carries that as GC-5. The declaration above says what
hyperphysics is *about*. That is a real constraint on what its ground must
eventually be — a ground for hyperphysics has to ground the mechanics, dynamics
and statics of operations, and not, say, the mechanics of bodies — but it is not
the ground.

`electrical.hm` is a **transport surface** rather than a layer of this field: it
states physical law about bodies and charge so adjacent fields can cite it. Under
this declaration, that file's relation to hyperphysics proper is now sayable. It
is a source of *forms* that the mechanics of operations may borrow, the same way
`metamathethicology.will_electrophysics` borrows from it. It is not an instance
of the subject matter declared above, and the file's own refusal of an `L0_`
prefix was already correct for a reason this declaration now sharpens.

## What this does not do

- **It does not discharge GC-5.** Naming a subject matter is not deriving a
  ground. GC-5 asks for a ground of which circuit theory would be a consequence;
  this file supplies neither a ground nor that consequence.
- **It does not make hyperchemistry or hyperbiology exist.** Both are declared
  and unwritten. No `.hm` file, package, axiom or check exists for either. A
  reader should treat both rows of the table as reserved names with stated
  scope, and nothing more.
- **It is not derived from anything in this repository.** The declaration is the
  author's statement of his own framework's scope. Nothing in `electrical.hm`,
  the package, or the tests entails it, and no check here bears on it.
- **It settles no placement question.** Where a *combination* field goes remains
  governed by the existing rule --- combination fields are hosted in
  `metamathethicology`, because neither field being combined can host a thing
  that requires both.
